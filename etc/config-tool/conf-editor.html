Edit the .conf files a Fugu MPPT reads at boot. Read them from a device, open a saved copy, or start from a board preset.

MQTT connects through the broker's WebSocket listener. Bluetooth and USB serial (115200 baud) need Chrome or Edge over https or localhost.
Files other than *.conf are kept and written back unchanged.
